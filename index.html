Clips

Board buttons

Use your MIDI keyboard's transport buttons: press Learn, then the button on the board.

    Settings

    Keys: Z–M hold the key's chords, ← → move the octave, Shift is the pedal, Space plays and stops, Enter takes a clip.

    Chordlight

    Lit keys are the notes worth playing next.

    • gold: land here
    • teal: in key, wander through
    • violet: leans; move the way its arrow points

    Play with the Q row: it plays the key's notes, and Q E T is the home chord.
