Clips

Board buttons

Use your MIDI keyboard's transport buttons: press Learn, then the button on the board.

    Settings

    Keys: Z–M hold the key's chords, ← → move the octave, Shift is the pedal, Space plays and stops, Enter takes a clip.

    Guitar

    experimental

    Saves what Chordlight hears from your guitar as a WAV file, to send with a report of notes it gets wrong.

    Amp

    If what you hear lags behind your playing, untick this and turn on your interface's direct monitoring, if it has it. Clicking the neck plays through the amp either way.

    Tuning

    Guitar mode is experimental. It hears one note at a time: single notes, riffs and melodies light up and record, but strummed chords don't yet. A note can be played in several places on the neck, so the one lit is a guess at where your hand is.

    Chordlight

    • teal: in key, wander through
    • violet: leans; move the way its arrow points
